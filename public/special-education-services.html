Our Services

Special education consulting and educational advocacy services are based on your child’s unique needs. Partners in Educational Advocacy can provide short or long-term consultative services.

Services may include:

  • Providing help navigating the special education eligibility process
  • Reviewing your student’s educational records and making recommendations for educational support and services
  • Facilitating communication with your child’s school team
  • Guiding you in requesting an initial evaluation to determine if your child is eligible for Special Education Services through an IEP (Individualized Education Plan)
  • Reviewing a school's initial or re-evaluation and helping parents prepare for an upcoming eligibility meeting
  • Attending IEP or 504 meetings
  • Consulting with your child's private providers
  • Observing your child when appropriate
