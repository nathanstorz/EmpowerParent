Partners in Educational Advocacy supports New Jersey families and schools in developing effective, personalized education plans for students with disabilities that ensure they receive a Free and Appropriate Public Education (FAPE). We assist families in navigating Individual Education Plans (IEPs), 504 plans, and behavioral supports every step of the way. We offer a truly integrated approach that ensures our services address the full spectrum of your child's needs by consultation with other professionals including speech, occupational, physical, and mental health therapists. Through collaborative consultation, data-informed decisions, and targeted support, we can help you advocate for your child's education, accessing the services and tools they need to succeed.

If you're a parent looking for direction we help turn challenges into meaningful educational outcomes, ensuring your child has the opportunity to succeed. As experienced Child Study Team members, we know how children learn, what helps them grow, and which supports truly make a difference.

Services

We Will:

  • Answer questions about the evaluation and IEP process and guide you throughout
  • Explain the Special Education Code (including special education classifications) and Parental Rights in Special Education
  • Help you articulate your questions throughout the process
  • Create a positive, collaborative working relationship between you and Child Study Team members
  • Provide the knowledge and support you need to effectively advocate for your child’s needs

Special education consulting and educational advocacy services are based on your child’s unique needs. Partners in Educational Advocacy can provide short or long-term consultative services.

Services may include:

  • Providing help navigating the special education eligibility process
  • Reviewing your student’s educational records and making recommendations for educational support and services
  • Facilitating communication with your child’s school team
  • Guiding you to develop an initial evaluation request to determine if your child is eligible for Special Education Services through an IEP (Individualized Education Plan)
  • Reviewing a school's initial or re-evaluation reports and helping you to prepare for an upcoming Eligibility Meeting / IEP Meeting
  • Attending IEP or 504 meetings
  • Consulting with your child's private providers
  • Observing your child when appropriate
